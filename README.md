## 1. About Our Business

**Mangal Tech Studio** is a managed creative visual production studio. We partner with small and growing businesses, direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands, Shopify store operators, and Instagram sellers to produce magazine-quality commercial product visuals without the friction, delays, and substantial expense of traditional physical photoshoots.

Traditionally, booking a commercial studio requires renting space, hiring lighting crews, buying props, styling sets, and shipping delicate inventory back and forth. Conversely, generic consumer AI image generators frequently hallucinate—distorting labels, smearing typography, and mangling product packaging.

Mangal Tech Studio bridges this divide through a **managed creative service model**. We do not simply hand customers access to automated software. Instead, our creative team takes ownership of the complete visual pipeline—combining brand-aligned art direction, state-of-the-art AI-assisted rendering, and meticulous manual artist quality review.

## 8. Quality & Product Accuracy

Product accuracy is an essential pillar of our creative process. When a customer purchases a product online, the delivered physical goods must reflect the digital imagery.

Our human review focuses strictly on:
* **Product Shape & Silhouettes:** Exact container curves, caps, nozzles, and dimensions preserved without warping.
* **Packaging Typography & Logos:** Brand names, typography, and logo placements kept sharp, aligned, and legible.
* **Chromatic Accuracy:** Calibrating product and packaging hues to represent authentic real-world shades.
* **Physical Proportions & Shadows:** True-to-life contact shadows and scale that look believable to customers.

> **Important Realism Note:**  
> AI-assisted image generation can sometimes alter fine or subtle product details when source reference images are blurry, dimly lit, or taken from a single angle. For optimal accuracy, clients are encouraged to provide clear reference photos captured from multiple angles. We do not claim that AI can accurately recreate unseen, obscured, or microscopic product details from a single photograph. Every final image is manually checked and polished before release.
