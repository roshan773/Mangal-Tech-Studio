## 7. Product Accuracy & Quality Assurance

Product fidelity is our absolute priority. When customers purchase online, the delivered physical item must match the digital visual representation.

### Our Multi-Point Quality Review Checklist:
* [x] **Product Silhouette & Shape:** Exact container curves, edges, and dimensions preserved without warping.
* [x] **Label & Typography Legibility:** Brand names, typography, logos, and certification marks kept crisp and accurate.
* [x] **Color Fidelity:** Product colors calibrated to represent real-life shades accurately.
* [x] **Surface Materials & Texture:** Glass reflections, matte coatings, metallic finishes, and paper textures rendered realistically.
* [x] **Physical Lighting Physics:** Natural shadow falloff, ambient occlusion, and directional reflections.

### Setting Realistic Expectations:
* Generative AI models can occasionally misinterpret fine details or complex geometric patterns when source references are blurry, low-resolution, or taken in poor lighting.
* For optimal visual precision, clients are encouraged to provide clear, well-lit reference photos captured from multiple angles (front, 45-degree angle, back, top).
* AI technology cannot invent or reconstruct obscured or unseen product features (such as tiny ingredient lists on the back of a bottle) from a single front-facing photograph.
* Every final image is manually checked and polished before release to ensure commercial readiness.
